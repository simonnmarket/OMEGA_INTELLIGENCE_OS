==================================================

FÓRMULAS PRINCIPAIS:

1. NET PROFIT CALCULATION:
   Net_Profit = (Best_Bid_Price - Best_Ask_Price) - 
                (Venue_A_Cost + Venue_B_Cost + Cross_Venue_Cost)

2. EXECUTION PROBABILITY:
   Execution_Prob = Avg(
     Ask_Venue_Confidence,
     Bid_Venue_Confidence, 
     Ask_Depth_Quality,
     Bid_Depth_Quality,
     Latency_Score
   )

3. POSITION SIZING:
   Position_Size = 
     Base_Size × Profit_Adjustment × 
     Liquidity_Adjustment × Probability_Adjustment ×
     Latency_Adjustment

4. RISK ADJUSTED SCORE:
   Risk_Score = 
     (Profit_Factor × 0.3) +
     (Execution_Prob × 0.3) +
     (Liquidity_Score × 0.2) +
     (Latency_Factor × 0.2)

FATORES DE AJUSTE:

• Profit Adjustment: Min(2.5, Profit × 15,000)
• Liquidity Adjustment: Liquidity score (0-1)
• Probability Adjustment: Execution probability (0-1)  
• Latency Adjustment: 1.5 - (Latency_Diff / 4.0)
