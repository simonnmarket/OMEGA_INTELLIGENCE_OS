What is an economic event?
Before we dive into the specifics, let's first understand what an economic event is and why it is relevant to trading. Economic events are public releases of information that have the potential to impact market prices. While some events may have a more specific effect on individual stocks or sectors, major news announcements can have sweeping effects on the entire market, including stocks, currencies, commodities, and interest rates. Even if economic news has little impact in the long run, data releases can often cause short-term volatility that day traders look to capitalize on.
