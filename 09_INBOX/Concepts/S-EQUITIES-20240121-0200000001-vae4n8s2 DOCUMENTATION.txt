ALGORITMOS DE VOLATILITY ARBITRAGE v7.0
==================================================

FÓRMULAS PRINCIPAIS:

1. CÁLCULO IV SKEW:
   IV_Skew = (IV_Puts_ATM - IV_Calls_ATM) / IV_Calls_ATM

2. CÁLCULO IV RANK:
   IV_Rank = Percentil(IV_Atual, IV_Histórico_252d)

3. EARNINGS IMPACT:
   Earnings_Impact = (IV_Atual - IV_Normal) / IV_Normal

4. TERM STRUCTURE:
   Term_Structure = (IV_Front_Month - IV_Back_Month) / IV_Back_Month

5. EXPECTED MOVE:
   Expected_Move = Preço * IV * √(Dias/365)

DECISÃO DE ESTRATÉGIA:

• SKEW > +15% → RISK_REVERSAL_SHORT
• SKEW < -15% → RISK_REVERSAL_LONG
• EARNINGS > 8% + IV_RANK > 70% → EARNINGS_STRADDLE  
• TERM_STRUCTURE > +10% → CALENDAR_SPREAD_SHORT
• TERM_STRUCTURE < -10% → CALENDAR_SPREAD_LONG
